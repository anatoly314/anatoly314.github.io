An interactive map of Israeli open data, built from public government datasets such as the CBS (Central Bureau of Statistics). It covers the socio-economic index, census 2022 measures, population and schools, at the level of municipalities, localities and statistical areas (neighborhoods). The UI is in Hebrew, English and Russian. Source code is at [github.com/anatoly314/israeli-gis](https://github.com/anatoly314/israeli-gis).
